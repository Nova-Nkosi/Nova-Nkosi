### Aspiring Financial Engineer | Accounting & Technology

I am an accounting student with a background in project coordination and technical accounting, working towards a career in financial engineering.

I am interested in where finance, technology, and problem-solving meet. As automation continues to change how accounting and finance work, I want to be part of building the systems behind that change - not just learning how to use them.

My focus is on developing strong financial fundamentals alongside practical technical skills, so I can build tools that are accurate, efficient, and genuinely useful for the people who rely on them.

Currently, I am building web-based products and learning how to apply programming to real financial problems - from data and reporting to automation and modelling.

I believe good financial tools should do more than calculate correctly. They should save time, reduce errors, and make complex work easier to understand.
